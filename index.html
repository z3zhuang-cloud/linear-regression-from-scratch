Same Salesperson, Different Signal

Student Researcher
Behavioral economics · Randomized experiment · N = 498

Extraversion, Appraisal, and Store Entry

  • Conducted a randomized online experiment under mentorship with 498 participants, comparing storefront images with and without an entrance salesperson.
  • Found that the entrance-salesperson condition reduced stated entry propensity by about 12 points on a 0–100 scale. Evidence was insufficient to support the original hypothesis that this effect varied with extraversion.
  • Combined regression analysis with coding of open-ended responses. Exploratory analyses linked higher extraversion to more positive and fewer negative interpretations of the salesperson; these associations do not establish a causal mechanism.
  • Presented the study at the Missouri Valley Economic Association (MVEA) 2026 Annual Meeting on November 6, 2026.
